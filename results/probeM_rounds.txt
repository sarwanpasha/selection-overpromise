probeM -- does the gap close over successive design rounds? 3 rounds, K=20 per round,
measured batch fed back into training each round, ridge surrogate, 5 seeds.

round  n_train  rho     raw_med   raw_mab  iso_mab  overpromise%
1      154      0.455  +0.2223   0.3023   0.1561   87.9
2      174      0.470  +0.1811   0.2612   0.0930   89.9
3      194      0.501  +0.1497   0.2218   0.0753   88.9

HEADLINE: iterating does not fix it. Across three rounds the median gap shrinks from
0.222 to 0.150, but the fraction of assays that overpromise stays flat near 89 percent.
A team running repeated design rounds keeps overpromising at the same rate, with only a
modest decline in magnitude.

Isotonic recalibration remains effective and improves as the calibration slice grows,
0.156 -> 0.093 -> 0.075.

CAVEAT, do not overstate: training-set size, rank accuracy and the reference level all
change together across rounds, so the modest shrink cannot be attributed to any one of
them. The flat overpromise rate is the robust observation.
