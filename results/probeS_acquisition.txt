Reading:
- every acquisition rule overpromises by about the same amount as greedy top-K, so the
  gap is not an artifact of greedy selection.
- isotonic recalibration removes most of it under every rule, winning 74 to 78 percent
  of assays at p near 1e-18.
- greedy captures the largest share of the achievable gain, 0.520, so the exploration
  rules trade immediate gain for coverage, as expected; Thompson sampling captures the
  least at 0.370 and also shows the smallest gap, consistent with a selection that is
  less concentrated at the top of the score.
