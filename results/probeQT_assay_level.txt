isotonic beats linear on 75 percent of assays, log10 p -16.
cross-fitted isotonic loses to plain isotonic (39 percent, log10 p -3).
tail-weighted isotonic wins against plain isotonic on 76 percent (log10 p -11) with the
same median absolute gap, so the two are equivalent in practice.
